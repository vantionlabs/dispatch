The p99 above is measured in this browser: every frame carries the instant the tick became ours, and the clock stops when this page has decoded it. It includes the flush interval, which is a deliberate wait rather than a cost we hid.

Coalesced ticks are prices a newer price replaced before either reached you. That is the design: a queued tick is a price that was true a minute ago, and showing it is worse than showing nothing.
